Creatine beyond the gym: what research says about strength, recovery and the brain

MX
MuscleX Editorial Updated today

Creatine monohydrate is one of the most studied supplements in sport. Most lifters know it for strength — but researchers are also looking at recovery, ageing and even brain health.

What it does in training

Creatine helps muscles regenerate energy during short, intense efforts like heavy sets and sprints. Combined with resistance training, it is consistently linked to small but real gains in strength and lean mass.

Key takeaways • A common daily dose is 3–5 g, taken any time of day
• A loading phase is optional — it just saturates muscles faster
• Early weight gain is mostly water stored in muscle

The brain question

Studies on memory and mental fatigue are promising but mixed, especially in well-rested, healthy adults. Effects appear larger under stress, like sleep loss. Expect more research before firm conclusions.

Educational content, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting supplements, especially if you have kidney concerns.
